Heal Through Service

Bend is hurting too. Let's heal together.

Since October 15, so many of us have asked the same quiet question, "What can I do?" Here is one answer. One of the ways a community heals is through acts of service, side by side. Bring your church, your youth group, your science class, your team, your service club or your family, and help Silver Rail Elementary students, teachers and families feel cared for by the whole town.

Volunteer opportunities in Bend for every kind of group.

Looking for a meaningful way for your group to give back in Bend, Oregon? You don't need a connection to Silver Rail to belong here. If your heart has been heavy since October, there's a place for you.

  • Churches and faith communities

    Host or cater a family gathering, open your fellowship hall, run a meal train or gift drive, or pray and write notes of encouragement for families and teachers.

    Great for: congregations, small groups, women's and men's ministries.

  • Youth groups and teens

    Serve at family meals and events, run a craft table with adults present, set up and clean up, assemble book boxes. Real service teens can be proud of, with volunteer hours documented.

    Great for: youth groups, Scouts, NHS, Key Club, sports teams.

  • School classes and clubs

    A science class, art class or student club can build activity kits, make cards and banners, or host a collection drive. Teachers, tell us your class and we'll design a project that fits.

    Great for: middle school, high school and COCC classes.

  • Spanish classes and bilingual volunteers

    Help welcome Spanish-speaking families at events and gatherings. Students practice real Spanish while families feel at home. A dignified loop where everyone gives and everyone receives.

    Se habla español · (858) 353-8268

  • Rotary, Kiwanis, Lions and service clubs

    Sponsor an experience for Silver Rail students, staff a signature event, or lend your members' professional skills to the fund's committees.

    Great for: service clubs, Chamber members, businesses with volunteer days.

  • Families who want to serve together

    Many Bend families want to volunteer on holidays and never find a spot in time. Sign up once and we'll invite you when a family-friendly role opens.

    Great for: parents with tweens and teens.
Silver Rail alumni

Once a Silver Rail student, always part of this family.

If you went to Silver Rail and you're in middle school or high school now, this is still your school, and it's okay that you're hurting too. You can help the kids walking the halls you walked. Greet families, serve at gatherings, help with crafts, or write a note to a student in the grade you were once in. Come with a parent or your group's adult leader, and tell us on the form that you're an alum.

How group volunteering works.

  1. Tell us about your groupUse the form below. Choose "General volunteer" and add your group's name, size, ages and when you're free.
  2. We match you to a real needOnly roles an upcoming event or family need actually calls for, so your time always counts.
  3. We get you readyClear instructions, a contact for the day, and any screening the role requires, arranged free.
  4. You serve, and we all heal a littleAfterward we'll share what your group made possible (never a child's name or story).
Keeping children safe and families' dignity first
  • Volunteers under 18 serve alongside a parent, guardian or their group's adult leader.
  • Adults who work directly with children without their parents present complete a free background check and two free trauma-informed trainings first. See the trainings.
  • Volunteers never see the family registry, and no photos of children are shared without a parent's signed release.
  • Every family is served with dignity. No one has to prove need, and no one is made to feel like charity.

Questions groups ask.

Can teenagers volunteer in Bend with the fund?
Yes. Middle and high school students are welcome, including Silver Rail alumni, alongside a parent, guardian or adult leader.
Can our church, youth group, class or club volunteer together?
Yes, that's what this page is for. Tell us your group's size and ages and we'll match you.
Do we need a background check or training?
Only for adults working directly with children without parents present. Many group roles, like serving at family meals, packing boxes or writing notes, don't require the trainings.
We can't volunteer in person. Can we still help?
Yes. Collection drives, notes of encouragement, and sponsoring an experience all help. See how businesses and groups give.

Silver Rail Children's Fund

A nonprofit started and run by Silver Rail Elementary parents. 100% of what comes in goes to the children who were at Silver Rail on October 15 — for what they need now, when it matters most, and, as funds allow, for years to come. The support follows the child, wherever they go to school next.

  • Parent-led, all volunteer
  • 100% to the kids
  • Every dollar posted monthly
  • Eligible until age 25
  • Fiscally sponsored by VictimsFirst, a 501(c)(3)
