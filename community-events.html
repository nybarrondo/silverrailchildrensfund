Community events for Silver Rail families.

Bend keeps showing up for our kids. These are events local businesses and neighbors are hosting to support Silver Rail families. Thank you to every one of them.

Sunday, November 15 · Doors 10 am · Volcanic Theatre, Bend

Tattoo Benefit for Silver Rail Elementary

Hosted by ZapTrip Tattoos and Volcanic Theatre. Donation-based entry, with tattoos, live music, aerial arts, fire dancers, kids activities, food vendors and more. The hosts are giving 100% of proceeds to Silver Rail families.

Sunday, November 22 · 4–7 pm · Base Camp, Bend

Sip and Shop at Base Camp

An evening of shopping and sipping hosted by Base Camp to support the Silver Rail Children's Fund.

Hosting something for Silver Rail kids?

We'd love to help spread the word. Email hello@silverrailchildrensfund.org with the date, place and where proceeds go, and we'll add it here. These events are organized by their hosts; the fund isn't running them, we're just passing them on.

Give to the Kids Fun Fund

Silver Rail Children's Fund

A nonprofit started and run by Silver Rail Elementary parents. 100% of what comes in goes to the children who were at Silver Rail on October 15 — for what they need now, when it matters most, and, as funds allow, for years to come. The support follows the child, wherever they go to school next.

  • Parent-led, all volunteer
  • 100% to the kids
  • Every dollar posted monthly
  • Eligible until age 25
