Kids Night Out · Friday, November 6 · Bend

A Friday night that's just for them.

Friday, November 6, 6:00 to 9:00 pm at Mountain Air Trampoline Park. Pizza, drinks, and the 360 obstacle course, with a whole floor of Silver Rail friends. Drop-off friendly, so grown-ups can take the evening too. Free for all Silver Rail kids, staff, and their families.

Three hours of jumping, climbing, laughing, and pizza with their own people. That's the whole plan, and for a Silver Rail kid right now it's a good one.

Drop-off friendly

  • Drop off at 6:00, pick up by 9:00. Mountain Air staff run the floor and supervise the kids under the park's own rules and insurance.
  • Kids must be 5 and up.
  • Kids are released only to the adults you list on the form, with photo ID.

Spots

  • 70 kids. Once we reach 70, registrations go on a waitlist in the order received, and we'll email you if a spot opens.
  • All Silver Rail kids, Silver Rail staff and teachers, and their families are welcome. Not registered with the fund yet? This form is all you need for this night.
  • Two quick steps: register with the fund here, then book Friday, November 6 on Mountain Air's ticket page. You'll get a confirmation email from us.

The waiver you're agreeing to when you register

I am the parent or legal guardian of every child named on this form. Mountain Air Trampoline Park runs this event under its own rules, staff, and insurance; its staff supervise the children. The Silver Rail Children's Fund arranges and pays for the event and checks families in; it does not supervise children. I understand trampolines and obstacle courses carry real risk of injury, and I accept that risk for my children. To the fullest extent the law allows, I release the Silver Rail Children's Fund and its directors, officers, and volunteers from claims arising from my children's participation, except for gross negligence or intentional harm. I authorize emergency medical care for my children if I cannot be reached. I will pick up my children by 9:00 pm. The fund may not photograph or name my children beyond what I chose on the form.

Silver Rail Children's Fund

A nonprofit started and run by Silver Rail Elementary parents. 100% of what comes in goes to the children who were at Silver Rail on October 15 — for what they need now, when it matters most, and, as funds allow, for years to come. The support follows the child, wherever they go to school next.

  • Parent-led, all volunteer
  • 100% to the kids
  • Every dollar posted monthly
  • Eligible until age 25
