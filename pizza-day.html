Pizza Day · Downtown Bend

Make a pizza with the chefs.

Deschutes Brewery is hosting Silver Rail kids and their families at the Public House downtown. Kids make their own pizza with the Deschutes chefs, make playdough, and play games led by Whimsy with Purpose. This is an in-kind donation: Deschutes is giving the space, the staff, the food, and the drinks. Whimsy with Purpose is giving the play. Families just show up.

Why pizza? Because for a child who was at Silver Rail on October 15, an unpressured afternoon with classmates, hands in dough, is not a break from recovery. It is recovery. Gentle, child-led play is how kids regain a sense of safety and say what they can't yet put into words.

60 spots each day, kids and adults together. Friday is full; Wednesday has room. A parent or guardian stays with their child the whole time. Siblings are welcome. If your day fills, you'll get an email with your place in line and can switch to the other day.

What happens
  • Each kid builds their own pizza at a prepped station, and the Deschutes kitchen bakes it.
  • Parents eat and drink too. Deschutes is feeding the grown-ups as well as the kids.
  • Playdough making, toys, and games led by Whimsy with Purpose while pizzas cook.
  • Costumes welcome, and a little Halloween on the tables — pumpkins to decorate, nothing scary. Quiet corner for anyone who needs a minute.
  • No photos of children without a parent's say-so. No press inside.

Thank you

Deschutes Brewery & Whimsy with Purpose

Deschutes Brewery is giving all of it as an in-kind donation to the Silver Rail Children's Fund, the Public House, the chefs' time, every pizza, and the drinks. Nothing about this day costs the fund a dollar. Whimsy with Purpose is bringing the playdough, the toys, and the games. Go say thank you.

Silver Rail Children's Fund

A nonprofit started and run by Silver Rail Elementary parents. 100% of what comes in goes to the children who were at Silver Rail on October 15 — for what they need now, when it matters most, and, as funds allow, for years to come. The support follows the child, wherever they go to school next.

  • Parent-led, all volunteer
  • 100% to the kids
  • Every dollar posted monthly
  • Eligible until age 25
