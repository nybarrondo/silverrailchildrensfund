From Silver Rail parents

Messages to Kaylub.

On October 15, Kaylub Giamanco — a Silver Rail dad with two daughters at the school — ran toward the gunfire. Our children came home that day. These are the messages Silver Rail parents want him to have.

Kaylub and his family have their own fund: Stand With Kaylub on GoFundMe. Please give there if you can.

What parents are saying.

Dear Kaylub,

I am writing this to thank you from the absolute bottom of my heart.

My daughter was out of her classroom for Walk to Read that morning when everything happened. She had nowhere to hide and no door to close. I cannot even bring myself to process what could have happened if you weren't right there.

Because of your incredible courage, swift action, and bravery, countless lives were saved — including my daughter's. You gave her back to us, and there are truly no words that can ever fully measure our gratitude to you.

We will forever hold you in our hearts, and we are praying for your continued strength and healing. Thank you for being her hero that day.

With endless gratitude,

Nicole Guerin-Ybarrondo · mom of 1st and 4th graders
