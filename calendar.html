Silver Rail Children's Fund

A nonprofit started and run by Silver Rail Elementary parents. 100% of what comes in goes to the children who were at Silver Rail on October 15 — for what they need now, when it matters most, and, as funds allow, for years to come. The support follows the child, wherever they go to school next.

  • Parent-led, all volunteer
  • 100% to the kids
  • Every dollar posted monthly
  • Eligible until age 25
  • Fiscally sponsored by VictimsFirst, a 501(c)(3)

Silver Rail Children's Fund · Bend, Oregon · hello@silverrailchildrensfund.org

Oregon public benefit nonprofit corporation, incorporated October 23, 2026 · Oregon Registry No. 264524597 · Tax ID 43-2607169

Fiscal sponsor. The Silver Rail Children's Fund is fiscally sponsored by VictimsFirst, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 32-0656956). Gifts made to VictimsFirst for the Silver Rail Children's Fund are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and VictimsFirst sends the tax receipt. 100% is passed through to the fund, with no administrative fee. How to give by check. Mail checks to Silver Rail Children's Fund, 61535 S Hwy 97, Ste 5-217, Bend, OR 97702.

Our own application for recognition of exemption under Section 501(c)(3) is in progress; if granted, exemption is retroactive to our incorporation date. You can also give on our Givebutter. We provide a receipt to every donor.
