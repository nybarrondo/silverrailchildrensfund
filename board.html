Board of Directors

Parents, volunteers, accountable in public.

Every person on this board is a volunteer. No one is paid. No one can approve support for their own child. The bylaws, the minutes, and every dollar in and out are published here. Titles marked interim are held until the board's first annual meeting, when officers are elected for full terms.

How the board governs.

Nobody is paid.Directors and officers serve without compensation. Out-of-pocket costs are reimbursed only with a receipt and advance approval.
Parents don't decide payments.An independent three-person Disbursement Committee — no Silver Rail parents, and no relatives or household members of one — approves every individual request. The board can't overrule it.
Our own kids get nothing extra.Board members' children receive only what every other Silver Rail kid receives. No individual requests while we serve, and not for a year after.
No money to parents, ever.Every payment goes to a provider or business for something a child receives. No cash grants. Why.
Two signatures on everything.Every disbursement is signed by two officers, neither of them the child's parent. The bank account is in the fund's own name and Tax ID.
Monthly public ledger.Every dollar in and every dollar out, by category, never by child, posted on this site.
Public bylaws and minutes.Posted here after adoption. The conflict-of-interest policy is signed annually by every director.
Trauma-trained.Every director completes NAMI TraumaInSight and the Trauma Informed Oregon modules. So does every volunteer who works with children. The trainings.
Independent voices.At least two directors with no eligible child, and a trauma-informed care advisory group that guides what we fund.

Silver Rail Children's Fund

A nonprofit started and run by Silver Rail Elementary parents. 100% of what comes in goes to the children who were at Silver Rail on October 15 — for what they need now, when it matters most, and, as funds allow, for years to come. The support follows the child, wherever they go to school next.

  • Parent-led, all volunteer
  • 100% to the kids
  • Every dollar posted monthly
  • Eligible until age 25
