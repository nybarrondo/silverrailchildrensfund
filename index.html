Why this fund exists

There was a gap. We filled it.

Bend has been extraordinarily generous. Funds for the families who lost someone go directly to those families, as they should. The school's PTO and the district each have funds for the school and the district. That is what they are for, and we're grateful for all of it.

But hundreds of children heard it, hid from it, and were walked out past it. Until now, there was no fund that put anything directly in their hands — not for a weekend, and not for the years ahead.

This fund started when Silver Rail parents tried to arrange a pumpkin patch trip for our kids — children who so desperately want to be kids again and play with their classmates. We weren't asking for a donation; we wanted to pay a local farm. We learned there was no fund that could do that. So we built one.

Eligibility is a roster, not a hardship essay. If a child was in the building that day, they're in.

A fund attached to a school ends when a child leaves it. This fund is attached to the child. A fifth grader who graduates in June, a family that transfers, a kid who moves across town — they're all still in, through middle school, high school, and college.

How it works

Three things, in order.

Now

Experiences & normalcy

The Silver Rail Kids Fun Fund: passes, memberships, kids' nights out, field trips — delivered to every household, no forms. Things a kid would want.

Next

Resources as needed

Gap coverage when other support runs out — counseling, tutoring if grades slip, the things that show up months and years later.

Later

Scholarships at 18

A scholarship waiting for each child when they're ready for college or trade school, wherever they've ended up.

Transparency

Look us up. We want you to.

This fund is run by parents of Silver Rail children. Directors serve without pay, abstain from any decision that would benefit their own household beyond what every family receives, and publish a monthly ledger of money in and money out.

Businesses are paid in full. Families never apply. No child's name or image is used without a parent's permission.
